## Welcome to Vanilla Finest

**Fast, lag-free vanilla-compatible pack** with a long view distance and stunning visuals. It is safe for your existing saves and forever worlds, and connects to many different vanilla server versions. Looks great without power-hungry shaders and runs well on macOS, too.

> ## Good to know
> - Press {key:iris.keybind.toggleShaders} to turn shaders on or off, {key:iris.keybind.shaderPackSelection} to choose one.
> - The **1** button next to Singleplayer opens the worlds of your original Minecraft launcher.
> - Hold {key:minihud:inventoryPreview} while looking at a chest or shulker box to see what's inside.

### Graphics
[Voxy](https://modrinth.com/mod/voxy) with Atmospherics gives beautiful visuals without shaders. For the best graphics pick a Complementary shader on the Look page; Bliss and Eclipse give a classic medieval mood.

### Adventure profile
Want more to explore? Start an adventure-focused world with Dungeons and Taverns and Grim Kingdoms: select the **Adventure** profile under More → Data Packs when creating a new world. Entirely optional, and only for new worlds.
